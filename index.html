🏴‍☠️ Catan Junior (Local Vue Edition)

Focused on Catan Junior rules: 1 die, lairs/ships, Coco tiles, market, and Ghost Captain.

Game Setup

Implemented Catan Junior rules
  • Single die roll each turn: 1-5 produce, 6 moves Ghost Captain and grants 2 resources from chosen island.
  • Ghost Captain blocks production on the island where it stands.
  • Build costs: ship = 1 goat + 1 wood, lair = 1 cutlass + 1 goat + 1 molasses + 1 wood.
  • Builds alternate between lair and ship (free-build Coco tile can bypass cost).
  • Coco deck and trading: marketplace (1x/turn) plus stockpile 2:1 trades.
  • Win immediately at 7 lairs including possible Spooky bonus for Coco leader.
